Tip

Press R for the rod. Hold the left mouse button to wind up, release to cast: the longer you hold, the farther it goes.

Watch the bobber. A few nibbles, then it's pulled under: click the left mouse button that moment to set the hook.

In a fight, hold to reel but keep the line tension in the green. Ease off when the fish runs, or the line snaps.

What bites depends on where you fish: the shallows, the reef, the deep water past the drop-off, and the time of day.

Joe at the fish stand by the pier buys your catch. Fish are worth more by weight, so bring the big ones in.

Marta at the boathouse sells upgrades: stronger line, a faster reel, a bigger hold, more fuel and a fish finder.

Press E at the boat to board, at the wheel to take the helm, and again to step away and walk the deck.

At the helm, the left mouse button fires your deck gun at the crosshair. It overheats, so fire in bursts.

Shoot a boat until it burns, pull alongside and press G to plunder its cash and fish. Your guns never run out of ammo.

The sea gets more dangerous the further you sail from the docks: Coastal Waters, then the Open Sea past the amber buoys, then the Deadwater past the red ones. Further out: tougher pirates, rarer fish, bigger plunder.

Save up for a new boat: an open workboat, a steel trawler, an old fishing launch, a fast sport-fisher, a deep-sea fishing ship, the WaveCutter speedboat or, for $2,500,000, a 40 m superyacht. They're in the Boats tab at Marta's or the harbour. Each boat is upgraded separately; your rod, reel and line go with you.

Attacking fishing boats and traders makes you wanted. From two stars the coast guard comes after you. Pirates carry a bounty.

Press U by the pier or at one of the sea stations and outposts for harbour services: repairs, upgrades, boats, and they buy your catch. They are all safe zones.

Whale-sized giant fish surface out at sea. Fit a harpoon, press Q at the helm to spear one from the stern, let it tire, and tow it into a harbour for a payout of up to $312,000. They are rare close in and common in the Deadwater.

The High Scores board is live: every captain ranked by lifetime earnings, with who is at sea right now. Press K in game.

Every fish has a rarity, from common to legendary: a legendary catch is worth $108,000 to $144,000. The rare ones are scarce near the docks and bite far more often the further out you sail.

Buy stern rod holders and up to three extra rods fish off the back of the boat while it is stopped, out in the Open Sea and the Deadwater. When one bends over, press B to grab it.

The Crimson Corsair, a pirate gunship, patrols the Open Sea (the orange skull on the minimap): a good first boss, worth $150,000. Press Esc for the menu: settings, or sail straight back to the home dock when you're out of a fight.

The Black Tempest, a pirate missile boat, roams the far side of the Deadwater (the purple skull on the minimap). Her big, slow missiles home on you: turn hard across one's path as it closes in, outrun it, or shoot it down. Sit still and it hits for 300. Sinking her pays $1,000,000.

After dark a sea monster, the Leviathan, rises in the north of the Deadwater (the green eye on the minimap). Her tentacles rise round your boat: when one rears up, steer out from under it, and shoot any that grabs you. Killing her pays $1,500,000.

The Iron Kraken, a pirate warship with 85,000 hp, patrols the Deadwater (the red skull on the minimap). Bring your best guns: she fires back, and sinking her pays $600,000. In multiplayer, team up and share the plunder.

Pirates hit harder the further out you go: buy more firepower in the Weapons tab. A bigger deck gun, gun cooling and targeting, and a bow cannon on the right mouse button keep them off your boat.

Hull armour and damage control keep you afloat in a fight. Armour adds hull points; damage control patches the hull by itself once the shooting stops.

Stern rod holders are steady income: up to three extra rods fish off the back of the boat while you're stopped out at sea. Add chum and better bait and they bite sooner and bring up rarer fish.

Upgrade your engine to outrun pirates and the coast guard. Each boat has its own engine, armour and weapons: a new boat starts with the basics.

Better bait & lures bring up rare, epic and legendary fish more often; chum makes every bite come sooner. Both work on your stern rods too.

Sell your catch before you take on pirates: a sunk boat loses what's in its hold. Harbour services and every outpost buy fish.

Press Space to pass a catch card or close a menu, and E near the wheel or in the middle of any boat to take the helm.

A trawl net fills with fish, crabs and lobster while you tow it slowly. Press N to haul it in, and set any dolphins, seals, turtles or manatees free before you keep the rest.

Sunken wrecks lie on the seabed out in the danger tiers. Your fish finder spots them; go over the side, dive with C and press E to open the chests. Better scuba gear means more air, and look out for sharks.

Too loud, or can't hear the sea for the engine? Press Esc and open the Audio tab: a volume for the sea and nature, your boat, fishing and the guns. M mutes it all.

On a phone or tablet: the left of the screen is a joystick, drag on the right to look, and the buttons fire, reel and use. ⋯ has the rest. Turn your phone sideways for the best view.

Press I for your cooler and the fish log. Deck lights from Marta let you fish after dark.
